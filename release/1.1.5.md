# Pomace 1.1.5

Pomace's interface moves to version 1.1 of its Carolina blue and navy design.

- Secondary buttons (Decompress, Stop, Done, Show details) share one bordered style, with a clear keyboard-focus outline and a stronger border when Increase Contrast is on.
- Warnings, failures and success messages use the design's own amber, red and green, tuned separately for light and dark appearance.
- The main pane uses the standard window background, and the canvas glow and panel highlights are quieter.

Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.
