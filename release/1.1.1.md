# Pomace 1.1.1

Pomace gains a refreshed native interface with adaptive Carolina blue and navy styling.

- Light and dark appearances use clearer panels, typography, and compression metrics.
- The scan summary fits the available window space without scrolling. Metrics reflow into compact rows when the schedule inspector is open or the window is narrow.
- Compression coverage, reclaimed space, and Compress / Decompress controls remain visible after a scan.
- Existing automatic updates, the bundled compressor, localized controls, keyboard navigation, and safety confirmations are preserved.

Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.
