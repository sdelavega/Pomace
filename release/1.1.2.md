# Pomace 1.1.2

Pomace adds per-folder insight: savings estimates, exclusions, and re-expansion reporting.

- Each watched folder now shows an estimate of the space compression would reclaim, sampled from real temporary copies so the estimate reflects your files and your chosen compressor.
- Folders can exclude files and subfolders by name pattern; excluded paths are skipped by scans and sweeps and labeled in the file table.
- A degradation indicator shows how much a folder has drifted since its last compression, so you can see when a re-compression is worthwhile.
- A re-expansion report attributes space reclaimed by decompression to your manual and scheduled actions.
- Scan history is pruned at launch and per folder, keeping the most recent runs for watched folders and dropping records for folders you no longer watch.
- Fixed scan cancellation, truncated runs, sweep scheduling and accounting bugs; improved schedule drift tolerance, error reporting, and accessibility labels for the exclusion field and existing controls.

Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.
